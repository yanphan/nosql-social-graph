QUERY: BT3-FRIEND-SUGGESTIONS
======================================================================
CYPHER:
MATCH (me:User {id:'usr_alice'})-[:FOLLOWS]->(f:User)-[:FOLLOWS]->(c:User) WHERE c.id <> me.id AND NOT (me)-[:FOLLOWS]->(c) AND coalesce(c.status,'active') <> 'locked' WITH me, c, count(DISTINCT f) AS mutual OPTIONAL MATCH (me)-[:INTERESTED_IN]->(t:Topic)<-[:INTERESTED_IN]-(c) WITH c, mutual, count(DISTINCT t) AS sharedTopics RETURN c.id AS id, c.username AS username, mutual, sharedTopics, mutual*2 + sharedTopics AS score ORDER BY score DESC LIMIT 10
